# FreedomForge Launch Checklist

## Pre-Launch (T-24h)

- Confirm production URL is healthy: `https://freedomforge.one`
- Confirm latest GitHub `main` is synced and tests are green
- Confirm Railway environment apply workflow has passed
- Confirm screenshots/logo files are available in this kit
- Confirm one primary CTA link for every channel

## Launch Hour (T-0)

- Publish X thread from `X-THREAD.txt`
- Publish community announcement from `COMMUNITY-POST.txt`
- Send media/investor email from `EMAIL-PITCH.txt`
- Post product launch note in Discord/Telegram
- Pin launch post and update profile link

## First 6 Hours

- Track incoming questions and route to FAQ responses
- Collect testimonials and quote-ready user feedback
- Monitor uptime and API/ops dashboards
- Repost best-performing thread/reel angle

## First 48 Hours

- Ship one visible product improvement
- Publish “what we learned” recap
- Refresh CTA with strongest social proof
- Prepare second-wave press outreach
